Official Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosures
✓ 100% On-Device DSP Audio • Zero Cloud Audio Upload

1. Zero Cloud Audio Processing (100% Local DSP)

When you record your singing take, the app captures audio for up to 15 seconds. Audio pitch detection (Yin DSP algorithm) and evaluation are calculated entirely on your local phone processor. Your voice recordings are NEVER uploaded, streamed, or sold to any cloud server or AI model.

2. Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Microphone access is strictly required to capture vocal frequencies while you sing. We never record audio in the background, and recording stops automatically after 15 seconds.

3. Local Storage & Complete Data Control

Takes and history are saved strictly in your device's private app cache. You can delete all history and audio files at any time via Settings ⚙️ > Clear All History 🗑️.

4. No Personal Tracking, No Logins

No user account, email address, or phone number is required. The app contains zero invasive trackers, tracking cookies, or advertising spy SDKs.
